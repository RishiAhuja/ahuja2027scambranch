Lists HF ids, parameter counts, family, median per-inference latency on the H100 MIG slice, and invalid free-text rates. SmolLM2-135M and TinyLlama-1.1B are daggered (100% invalid). Granite is `ibm-granite/granite-3.1-1b-a400m-instruct` (MoE; ~1.33B params).

### zeroshot_quality.tex / zeroshot_branch.tex
Qwen2.5-1.5B leads full-call macro-F1 (~.73). Opening-only disagreement is ~0 (identical openings); adding the first reply raises disagreement sharply (.30–.80). Mean reaction deltas are often negative (first reply lowers P(fraud) on average) but mood-specific flips remain large.

### by_language.tex
Hindi–English typically outperforms Tamil–English; gaps are often driven by false alarms.

### by_mood.tex
For several strong models (e.g., Qwen2.5-1.5B, Gemma-3-1B), suspicious recipients raise false alarms while cooperative victims depress fraud recall—evidence that the detector reads the victim.

### caller_only.tex
Hiding recipient turns does not remove sibling disagreement; caller responses after the branch still leak stance.

### streaming.tex
Qwen2.5-1.5B alerts early (negative mean delay, high pre-evidence alert rate); Qwen3-1.7B is more conservative (higher miss rate, positive mean delay).

### ft_*.tex
Fine-tuning lifts macro-F1 into the mid-.80s for 1.5B and mid-.70s for 0.5B; branched vs unbranched differences sit inside seed variation. Consistency (lambda=1) does not reliably beat branched.

### break_points.tex
Hardest families include keyword-confound legitimates (OTP in delivery, IFSC requests) and hard fraud misses; reaction flips concentrate under suspicious replies for several models.

## Method notes

- Bootstrap: resample 12 scenario families with replacement, 10,000 reps, seed 42; recompute metrics with fixed dev thresholds.
- Two-sided bootstrap $p = 2\min(P(\mathrm{diff}\le 0), P(\mathrm{diff}\ge 0))$.
- Holm correction applied within each test family (T1–T5) separately.
- McNemar (exact binomial) where paired per-call or paired-sibling decisions apply; see `stats.json`.
- T5 pools seeds by averaging the metric inside each bootstrap replicate; seed range is reported separately. **3 seeds × 12 families is low power.**
